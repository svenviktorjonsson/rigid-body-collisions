# Exact-law pressure and contact-face recovery

The final isolated native helper recovers all four new velocity captures (45, 48, 231, and 321 rows) under the unchanged circular Coulomb law. Independent full-matrix projection residuals are 1.47e-9, 9.40e-15, 8.87e-14, and 1.01e-11 m/s, below the original 1e-8 threshold. Each also passes independent nonnegative-pressure, disk, maximum-dissipation, and finite passive-energy checks. The independent mechanics reviewer recomputed these gates separately. This is captured-system recovery, not a completed trajectory or an accuracy qualification.

`pressure_release.h` separately recovers the 54-row zero-friction split-position capture. Releasing two stale normal-pressure rows produces the original projection residual 5.74e-9 in 16 small normal SVDs. The independent reviewer found a three-row release with residual 2.92e-17. The normal problem is feasible; the rejected iterate inherited a stale pressure face. Normal-only pressure guides for frictional velocity systems are numerical initializers and never physical output.

The production candidate files are `coulomb_active.h`, `coulomb_active_trust.h`, and `pressure_release.h`. The wrapper starts with warm positive-pressure contacts, keeps their normal and both tangent rows, and solves the original principal mobility matrix. It zero-expands a candidate and evaluates every row of the full original contact matrix. A violated omitted normal triggers expansion. Stalled searches try bounded individual contact releases. The final gate always retains the original matrix, right-hand side, friction coefficients, upper bounds, contact tolerance, and finite passive-energy bound.

An additional numerical mode guess handles a weak sticking/sliding manifold: when normal complementarity already passes but an interior friction disk has excessive tangential velocity, the contact closest to its disk boundary receives an opposing-slip boundary starting guess. This guess is used only during numerical search; the accepted impulse must still solve the original full law. For the 45-row case, the combination of this guess and subsequent pressure-face ranking succeeds; plain release and plain Newton search fail. The final-friction search now tests the original physical gate at each iteration rather than requiring the stricter intermediate Fischer–Burmeister merit target.

Bounds are 4096 full rows, 384 reduced rows, eight subset passes, aggregate 512 Newton iterations, 256 main SVDs, 512 damped steps, 96 friction-continuation attempts, and 128 pressure-face attempts (at most 128 additional pressure SVDs). Normal-only pivot guides are limited to eight calls; Bullet does not expose an internal pivot-count limit. The pressure-face search is incomplete and a decline does not establish nonexistence. Failed searches leave the caller's physical output unchanged.

`checks.cpp` verifies unilateral pressure release, redundant pressure, infeasible-normal rejection with unchanged output, and a 450-row problem where an omitted compressing contact must be added before the full gate passes. The first test used an invalid exact-float assertion; its correction changes only the test and is disclosed in `check-test-erratum.txt`.

`active-final-four-provenanced.jsonl`, `final-source.zip`, and `final-provenance.json` freeze the final helper, supporting source, capture hashes, native flags, pinned Bullet revision, and static-library/binary hashes. Earlier prototypes, unsuccessful mode guesses, and failed searches are retained as exploratory negative evidence. Their partial counters and optimizer messages are not physics certificates. The interrupted six-trajectory study is retained separately with four completed rejections and two interrupted identities; it remains incomplete and unqualified. Fresh integrated trajectories are still required.
