## Recommendation

Keep `early_component_recovery=False` as the default. The isolated evidence supports exposing a carefully documented experimental option after root integrates and tests its API/provenance. The option uses actual failed seeds, preserves original physics/tolerances and applies only fully gated impulses. It does not establish that alternative accepted roots produce equivalent body histories.

Root should freeze the next held-out full-history plan before evaluating any combined target or geometry revision. Preserve solver/accuracy rejections and all energy/work ledgers; report original versus optional schedule root and trajectory changes. Do not promote this option as a universally faster, converged, novel physical or hard real-time method based on captured systems. Root alone integrates, commits and pushes.
