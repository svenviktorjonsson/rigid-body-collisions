Deformation model to investigate

Resolve normal indentation, tangential shear and asymmetric pressure/rocking with passive internal modes and a distributed contact patch. Derive the independent angular impulse from the traction moment. Validate force reversals and vibration decay before outgoing spin. This is a supported next model, not an already demonstrated accuracy improvement.
