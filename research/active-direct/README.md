# Bounded numerical contact restarts

This archive recovers the three new velocity failures from the source-95d224f full-history study by changing numerical search only. The unmodified mobility A, free-velocity target b, circular Coulomb coefficient, unilateral normal complementarity, original 1e-8 m/s projection gate, and finite passivity gate remain authoritative. Intermediate coefficients, forces, pressure gauges and Newton damping are never applied to bodies.

The final source is `final/`. Root production integration is separate: the supplemental fallback is initially enabled only for systems of at most 64 rows and only after the previously available solver declines. Native LAPACK is an explicit optional numerical dependency; this is not evidence of WebAssembly portability or authentic-material calibration.

`circular_restart::solve(A,b,x,hi,dep,tolerance,stats,svd_limit=1024)` requires fresh per-call statistics; the engine should aggregate them after returning. It leaves x unchanged on every rejection. Input dimensions, finite values and normal/tangent dependencies are checked before indexing. Direct warm/cold Fischer–Burmeister searches handle the 60- and 51-row failures. For the 39-row failure, a normal-guide continuation produces a stalled force distribution. An SVD of the mobility columns of candidate contacts supplies a basis-invariant numerical null projector. Among unresolved interior tractions, the largest traction/capacity ratio is tried first. The projected displacement toward opposing traction is multiplied by the fixed fractions 1/4, 1/2, 1 and 2, followed by an opposing-cap initial guess and an original-law Moré trust-region search. At most two contact modes are tried.

The final neutral projector uses the existing one-sided Jacobi routine. The nonlinear trust step uses LAPACK DGESDD and retains weak numerical directions. Numerical SVD outputs and final physical impulses are checked separately. Recorded mobility changes of the successful neutral guides are approximately 1e-16 m/s; a truncated null projector is a numerical guide, not an exact additional physical constraint.

For systems up to 64 rows, direct, homotopy, projector and nonlinear restarts share at most 1024 SVD calls; larger supplemental systems have a 256 limit and only the direct branch. At most 2048 attempted outer numerical steps are permitted by the fixed phase bounds (the actual combined upper bound is smaller). The normal pressure initializer has a separately disclosed limit of 128 pressure SVD attempts and at most one Dantzig guide call; Dantzig's internal pivot count is not exposed or bounded by this interface. Numerical damped linear solves in the direct and homotopy phases have separate 512 limits each. These limits are additional to the original solver's work. Requesting fewer than 256 supplemental SVD calls declines without searching.

`final/validate.py` builds the guarded helper and controls, then replays 20 distinct exact archived contact systems against a frozen copy of the prior solver. It independently recomputes the original projection, disk/bound, complementarity and energy gates in Python. The library bytes, compiler, flags, input hashes and source hashes are recorded in `final/provenance.json`. This proves captured-system recovery, not the completion or accuracy qualification of a fresh hull trajectory. New full-history evidence requires a prospective source and protocol checkpoint.

## Retained rejected searches and scope corrections

All exploratory variants are retained. `first-three-native.jsonl` established direct recovery of 60/51 but rejected 39. The initial minimum-norm and portable TRF variants, increased small-system budgets and fixed-stage homotopy did not solve 39. `39-more-homotopy12-native.jsonl` exhausted 1024 SVD calls after ordering modes by residual, demonstrating why aggregate budget and mode ranking matter. `39-original-neutral-fully-portable-native.jsonl` records the completely Jacobi-based trust search rejecting at approximately 2.48e-8, while the Jacobi projector with DGESDD trust search succeeds. Both positive and negative evidence are retained.

Files whose names contain `derived-initial`, `independent-initial`, or `pre-restart-neutral-f` are diagnostic modified initial guesses, with the physical matrix/target/coefficient unchanged. They are not independent original-input engine replays. The final combined replay starts with the original archived impulse and derives its own guide; it does not use an archived accepted answer.

Early `candidate_trf*` receipts undercounted unsuccessful restart SVD work by recording only the successful branch's count. They are exploratory receipts, not certified aggregate-budget results. Later Moré candidate receipts count all attempts; their 7334-call exploratory total exceeds the production supplemental cap and is not used as a production performance claim. Final wrapper receipts count all configured phases.

`39-candidate-more-lapack-native.jsonl` is empty because the initial link command used unavailable development symlinks (`-llapack -lblas`); no physical solve occurred. `final/39-build-declined-empty.jsonl` likewise records no physical run: compiling the frozen prior baseline with the new helper's strict warning flag encountered an unrelated prior header indentation warning. The validated command treats frozen third-party/prior baseline includes as system headers while retaining strict warnings for new sources. Neither empty file is a success or rejected physics case.

The linear fixed-mode proof trial in `39-linear-mode-proof.json` failed the original gate and is not used. Rank seven of its target-velocity equations also disproves the exploratory assumption that the contact component behaved as a single six-degree-of-freedom body. The exact contact component may contain several bodies.
